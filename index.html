Medical Image Segmentation

Healthcare

Doctors can spend weeks to months to manually segment kidney vasculature from high-resolution 3D HiP-CT scans. We developed an automated segmentation system using U-Net architecture to analyze kidney vasculature from those high-resolution 3D HiP-CT scans. The model achieves 97% accuracy, reducing segmentation time from months to hours per dataset. This supports helps the professionals to focus on the more of their ressources on more complex parts of the kidney while sparing time.
