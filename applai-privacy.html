Applai Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Applai processes, why we use it, which types of service providers may process it, and the choices available to you.

1. About Applai

Applai is operated by Mirai Engineering OÜ.

Applai is a candidate-side job-search platform designed to help users discover job opportunities, prepare and tailor application materials, track applications, and automate parts of the application process.

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is processed when you use Applai.

2. Information We Process

Account Information

When you create or use an Applai account, we may process information such as:

  • your name
  • email address
  • authentication identifiers
  • account ID
  • account settings and preferences
Career Profile and Resume Information

To provide Applai's core functionality, you may provide information including:

  • employment history
  • education
  • skills
  • qualifications
  • professional projects
  • certifications
  • languages
  • job titles
  • career preferences
  • CV or resume content
  • professional achievements
  • contact information contained in uploaded documents
  • other professional information you choose to provide

You should avoid including personal information in your CV or profile that is not necessary for your job search.

Job Search and Application Information

When you use Applai, we may process information relating to your job search, including:

  • job postings
  • job descriptions
  • job URLs
  • companies
  • roles
  • saved jobs
  • application status
  • application dates
  • interview status
  • notes
  • application answers
  • other information needed to assist with an application
AI and Conversational Content

When using AI-assisted features, Applai may process:

  • prompts
  • messages
  • resume instructions
  • job descriptions
  • generated resumes
  • suggested edits
  • application answers
  • cover-letter content
  • other information necessary to perform the requested task

Some information may be sent to third-party AI providers to generate the requested output.

Auto Apply Information

If you use Auto Apply, currently offered as a Beta feature, Applai may process information necessary to assist with completing application forms.

This may include information from:

  • your Applai profile
  • your resume
  • saved application answers
  • job preferences
  • application materials

Auto Apply is intended to assist with applications selected or authorized by you.

You remain responsible for ensuring that information submitted on your behalf is accurate.

Payment Information

Payments and subscription management may be handled by Stripe.

Applai may receive limited information relating to your purchase or subscription, such as:

  • customer identifier
  • subscription or plan
  • transaction status
  • payment status
  • billing status
  • renewal or cancellation status

Applai does not store your complete payment-card number.

Technical Information

We may process limited technical information required to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain Applai.

This can include:

  • IP address
  • browser information
  • device information
  • session information
  • application logs
  • error information
  • security information
  • feature usage information

3. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • create and manage your account
  • maintain your career profile
  • generate and tailor resumes
  • analyze job descriptions
  • identify relevant parts of your professional background
  • provide job-search functionality
  • manage saved jobs
  • track applications
  • generate application materials
  • provide Auto Apply functionality
  • process paid access to Applai
  • provide customer support
  • maintain the security of the service
  • investigate errors
  • prevent abuse
  • maintain and improve Applai
  • comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. AI Processing

Applai uses third-party AI systems to provide some functionality.

Information required to perform an AI-assisted task may be sent to an AI provider. Depending on the requested feature and the service configuration, Applai integrates with AI services provided by Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google.

We aim to send only the information reasonably necessary to perform the requested task.

Applai does not sell your:

  • resume
  • career profile
  • prompts
  • application history
  • application answers

to advertisers or data brokers.

AI-generated results may be inaccurate and should be reviewed before being used in a real application.

5. Candidate-Side Use

Applai is built for job candidates.

Applai may analyze your background and compare it with job descriptions in order to help you prepare your own applications.

Applai does not make hiring decisions on behalf of employers.

Any:

  • recommendation
  • match
  • analysis
  • resume suggestion
  • generated content

is intended to assist your own decision-making and does not represent an employer's assessment of your suitability.

6. Service Providers

We use third-party providers to operate Applai. The current service integrations include:

  • Clerk for authentication and account access
  • Stripe for checkout, subscription payment processing, and subscription management
  • Railway for application hosting and infrastructure
  • MongoDB for account, profile, application, and related service data
  • PostgreSQL infrastructure for job-market data
  • Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google model services for AI-assisted features, depending on the requested feature and current configuration

These providers may process information only as needed to perform the services they provide to Applai.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Applai and operate the service.

Account and career-profile information may generally be retained while an account remains active.

If a user deletes their account or requests deletion, personal information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period.

Some information may temporarily remain in:

  • backups
  • security logs
  • infrastructure logs

Certain records may also be retained where required by applicable law.

8. International Processing

Applai uses internet-based infrastructure and service providers that may process information in countries other than the country where you live.

Where applicable data-protection law requires safeguards for international transfers, we rely on appropriate mechanisms used by our relevant service providers.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect user information.

These may include:

  • authenticated access
  • encrypted network connections
  • restricted access
  • infrastructure security
  • operational monitoring

No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

These may include the right to:

  • access your data
  • correct inaccurate information
  • request deletion
  • restrict certain processing
  • object to certain processing
  • receive certain information in a portable format
  • withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis
  • make a complaint to the relevant data-protection authority

To make a privacy request, contact:

contact@mirai-engineering.com

11. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Applai account and associated personal information by contacting:

contact@mirai-engineering.com

12. Third-Party Websites

Applai may display or link to:

  • job postings
  • employer websites
  • recruitment systems
  • other third-party websites

Those third parties operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Applai is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

13. Age Requirement

Applai is intended for users aged 18 or older.

We do not knowingly provide Applai accounts to children.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Applai changes.

When we make material changes, we may update the effective date and provide additional notice where appropriate.
