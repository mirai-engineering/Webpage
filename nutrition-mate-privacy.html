Nutrition Mate Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 18, 2026

Nutrition Mate ("we," "our," or "us") provides tools for nutrition tracking, food logging, recipe storage, weight tracking, and AI-assisted food entry. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

a. Account Information

When you create or use an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers such as your account ID

b. Profile and Health-Related Information

To provide the app's features, we may collect information you enter, including:

  • Age
  • Sex
  • Height
  • Weight
  • Weight goals
  • Calorie and macronutrient goals
  • Activity and onboarding information

c. Nutrition and App Content

We may collect information you create or enter in the app, including:

  • Food logs
  • Custom foods
  • Recipes
  • Recipe ingredients and instructions
  • Favorites and saved entries
  • Weight log history

d. Voice, Camera, and Photos

If you grant permission, the app may access:

  • Microphone input for voice-based food logging
  • Camera for nutrition label scanning or image capture
  • Photo library for selecting images to scan or process

Audio, images, and text you submit may be processed to provide app features such as transcription, food extraction, and recipe parsing.

e. Purchase and Subscription Information

If you subscribe through Apple, we may receive and store subscription entitlement information such as:

  • Subscription status
  • Product identifier
  • Original transaction identifier
  • Expiration or trial dates
  • Auto-renewal status

We do not receive your full payment card details. Payments are processed by Apple.

f. Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the app, such as:

  • App version
  • Device-related identifiers used for app functionality and sync
  • Database/client identifiers
  • Error and diagnostic information

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Personalize calorie, macro, and weight goal calculations
  • Save and sync your food logs, recipes, favorites, and profile data
  • Provide AI-assisted food logging and recipe parsing
  • Process voice, image, and text inputs you choose to submit
  • Manage subscription access and verify premium entitlement
  • Improve app reliability, security, and performance
  • Diagnose bugs and prevent abuse

How Information Is Stored and Shared

Your data may be stored locally on your device and, where applicable, synchronized with our backend infrastructure to support account access, syncing, and subscription entitlement checks.

We may share information with service providers that support core app functionality, such as:

  • Authentication providers
  • Cloud database and backend infrastructure providers
  • Apple, for subscription and payment processing
  • AI or model-processing services, where needed to provide requested features

We do not sell your personal information.

Tracking and Advertising

Nutrition Mate is not designed as an advertising platform. Based on the current app design, we do not use your health or nutrition data for third-party advertising.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Permissions

The app may request access to:

  • Microphone
  • Camera
  • Photos

These permissions are optional, but certain features will not work without them.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the app and maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

If you stop using the app, some information may remain in backups or records for a limited period where reasonably necessary.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Nutrition Mate is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your information
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Withdraw certain permissions on your device

You can also manage subscription billing and cancellation through your Apple account settings.

International Use

If you use the app outside the country where our services are operated, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions where privacy laws may differ.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
