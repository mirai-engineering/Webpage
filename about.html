The Problem We Solve

Mirai delivers AI-engineering world-wide, and its based in Europe. It was founded with a vision to democratize AI technology. After experiencing from first hand the lack of knowledge in many big companies when implementing AI solutions in a sustainable way, we saw an opportunity to bridge the gap between complex machine learning algorithms and real-world business applications.

Our Vision

Having been at the forefront of the AI revolution since its inception, we've witnessed the critical gap between AI's potential and its practical implementation. Companies struggle with unrealistic expectations for AI engineering roles that barely existed before 2022, while lacking clear pathways for in-house AI adoption.

We envision a future where experience meets realistic expectations, where organizations can seamlessly integrate AI into their operations without confusion. By bridging this gap, we can unlock AI's true transformative potential.

Leadership & Core Values

Bruno Copa

CEO & Founder

Bruno's journey began before the boom of the AI revolution. Long before ChatGPT or Gemini became household names, Bruno was already pioneering the use of artificial neural networks in the field of combustion engineering. After earning both his B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Engineering at RWTH Aachen University, he worked across projects in the aviation and automotive sectors, implementing AI & ML solutions both in-house and in-cloud native-environments. His experience spans across leading international companies such as Airbus and AVL. He's contributed to key projects at Porsche, Cariad, and led transformative collaborations with MIT.

Bruno's vision: "Artificial Neural Networks are the second most powerful form of intelligence we've encountered, just after human intelligence. I believe AI will always be a tool to enhance our productivity and deepen our understanding of the world, yet the foundations of the world can only be fully grasped through the abstraction capabilities unique to humans. It's a double-edged sword: While AI-powered tools enhance & boost our productivity, if we misuse it, we risk harming our own human neural networks. Let's use this gift of the 21st century responsibly."
