Nutrition Mate Privacy Policy

Nutrition Mate

Effective Date: March 18, 2026

Nutrition Mate ("we," "our," or "us") provides tools for nutrition tracking, food logging, recipe storage, weight tracking, and AI-assisted food entry. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

Information We Collect

a. Account Information: name, email address, and authentication identifiers such as your account ID.

b. Profile and Health-Related Information: age, sex, height, weight, weight goals, calorie and macronutrient goals, and activity or onboarding information you enter.

c. Nutrition and App Content: food logs, custom foods, recipes, recipe ingredients and instructions, favorites, saved entries, and weight log history.

d. Voice, Camera, and Photos: if you grant permission, the app may access microphone input, camera capture, and photo-library selection. Audio, images, and text you submit may be processed for transcription, food extraction, and recipe parsing.

e. Purchase and Subscription Information: subscription status, product identifier, original transaction identifier, expiration or trial dates, and auto-renewal status. Payments are processed by Apple, and we do not receive your full payment card details.

f. Device and Technical Information: app version, device-related identifiers used for app functionality and sync, database or client identifiers, and error or diagnostic information.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to create and manage your account, personalize calorie, macro, and weight goal calculations, save and sync your food logs and profile data, provide AI-assisted food logging and recipe parsing, process voice, image, and text inputs you choose to submit, manage subscription access, improve app reliability and performance, and diagnose bugs or prevent abuse.

How Information Is Stored and Shared

Your data may be stored locally on your device and, where applicable, synchronized with our backend infrastructure to support account access, syncing, and subscription entitlement checks.

We may share information with service providers that support core app functionality, such as authentication providers, cloud database and backend infrastructure providers, Apple for subscription and payment processing, and AI or model-processing services where needed to provide requested features. We do not sell your personal information.

Tracking, Permissions, and Retention

Nutrition Mate is not designed as an advertising platform. Based on the current app design, we do not use your health or nutrition data for third-party advertising.

The app may request access to the microphone, camera, and photos. These permissions are optional, but certain features will not work without them.

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide the app and maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. If you stop using the app, some information may remain in backups or records for a limited period where reasonably necessary.

Security, Children, and Your Choices

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Nutrition Mate is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access your information, correct inaccurate information, request deletion of your information, and withdraw certain permissions on your device. You can also manage subscription billing and cancellation through your Apple account settings.

International Use and Changes

If you use the app outside the country where our services are operated, your information may be processed in other jurisdictions where privacy laws may differ.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at contact@mirai-engineering.com.
