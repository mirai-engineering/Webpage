About Mirai Engineering

Mirai delivers AI-engineering world-wide, and its based in Europe. It was founded with a vision to democratize AI technology. After experiencing from first hand the lack of knowledge in many big companies when implementing AI solutions in a sustainable way, we saw an opportunity to bridge the gap between complex machine learning algorithms and real-world business applications.

Leadership

Bruno Copa

CEO & Founder

Bruno's journey began before the boom of the AI revolution. Long before ChatGPT or Gemini became household names, Bruno was already pioneering the use of artificial neural networks in combustion engineering. After earning both his B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Engineering at RWTH Aachen University, he worked across projects in aviation and automotive sectors, implementing AI & ML solutions both in-house and in cloud-native environments.

His experience spans leading international companies such as Airbus and AVL. He's contributed to key projects at Porsche, Cariad, and led transformative collaborations with MIT.

Bruno's vision: "Artificial Neural Networks are the second most powerful form of intelligence we've encountered—just after human intelligence. I believe AI will always be a tool to enhance our productivity and deepen our understanding of the world. Let's use this gift of the 21st century responsibly."
